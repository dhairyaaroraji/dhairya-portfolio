DEEP DIVE CASE STUDY

JalSetu Strategy & Technical Blueprint

Team NOVASTACKS • Shortlisted for Code4Nature Challenge 2026

The Problem

India faces severe groundwater depletion and regional water stress. Traditional infrastructure depends heavily on wells, borewells, rivers, and water tankers. Ground water reserves are finite and rapidly diminishing in critical zones.

The Idea

JalSetu explores an abundant alternative source: Atmospheric Moisture. However, Atmospheric Water Generators (AWGs) cannot be deployed blindly—their efficiency depends heavily on local atmospheric conditions. JalSetu answers the critical question: "Where should AWG capacity be deployed for maximum water-security impact?"

How It Works (Visual Flow)
01
COLLECT DATA: Ingest local data including groundwater conditions, humidity, rainfall, temperature, and water stress.
02
ANALYZE: Execute data processing and prediction algorithms to evaluate atmospheric suitability.
03
RANK LOCATIONS: Identify high water-stress regions that possess sufficient ambient humidity.
04
RECOMMEND: Output recommended location, AWG capacity, and deployment priority.
05
DEPLOY: AWG hardware units are deployed based on intelligence rankings.
06
MONITOR: Microcontroller & IoT sensors monitor humidity, temperature, tank level, and operating conditions.
07
LEARN: Field data feeds back into the engine to continuously improve recommendation models.
AWG Technology & Physical Architecture

The hardware extracts drinking water from moisture in ambient air by cooling humid air below its dew point, condensing vapor into liquid water, and subjecting it to multi-stage filtration.

Physical System Components:
Air Intake + Pre-Filter → Compressor + Cooling Coil → Condensation Chamber → Storage Tank + Level Sensor → Carbon Filtration → UV Sterilization → Mineralization → Microcontroller → Solar Panel + Battery

Proof-of-Concept Prototype (Demonstrator):

A low-cost benchtop prototype can demonstrate condensation principles using a Peltier module, heat sink, fan, metal condensing plate, collection tray, and a 12V power source (approximate demo cost: ₹800–₹1,500).

Differentiation & Positioning
WE DON'T BUILD JUST THE MACHINE.
WE BUILD THE INTELLIGENCE THAT TELLS IT WHERE TO GO.

Established hardware manufacturers (e.g., Watergen, IICT Meghdoot, Acqua Air) produce AWG units. JalSetu does not aim to replace hardware manufacturers—instead, it provides the deployment intelligence layer that ensures these machines are installed where they achieve maximum operational efficacy.

LOCAL DATA → MORE DEPLOYMENTS → MORE FIELD DATA → BETTER PREDICTIONS → BETTER DEPLOYMENT → STRONGER DATA ADVANTAGE

Expected Impact
  • Reduces dependence on depleted groundwater in ecologically suitable regions.
  • Establishes an evidence-based framework for deploying atmospheric water generation.
  • Eliminates blind infrastructure capital expenditure by leveraging predictive local data.
  • Provides scalable drinking-water alternatives to rural and water-stressed communities.
